devlog, Q3 2026

2026-10-29

introduction

after the first half of 2026 devlog i wanted to keep doing this, so here is the third quarter, july until september. again i looked through my public github activity, and this time there is a clear theme: LNURLcash.

github commit search shows 788 public commits from my account in Q3, about three times the whole first half. the biggest chunks were lnurl-wallet, bearlett, lnurl-mint and lnurl-server.

LNURLcash

most of the quarter went into LUD-25, bearer notes on top of LNURL-withdraw. it started in july as a draft plus two small projects, a wallet and a mint, and by the end of september it was a github org with around twenty repos.

lnurl-wallet alone got well over 250 commits and lnurl-mint about 110. in august the spec got a serious review from people building their own implementations, in september the whole thing was rewritten around taproot output keys, and at the end of september the HD derivation and Lightning Address part was split into its own LUD-26. i wrote a separate post about all of that: LNURLcash: LUD-25 and LUD-26, and everything around it.

around the spec a lot of smaller things appeared: lnurlcash-core in rust, client libraries for typescript, python, go and kotlin, a conformance suite, a libbitcoinkernel wrapper (kernel), a DLC oracle, a Core Lightning mint plugin, the raffle ticket generator and the lnurlcash.com website.

bearlett

the second biggest repo was bearlett, a bearer asset wallet for LNURLcash and Cashu, with independent wallet and notes napplets. over 200 commits. it's nice to see bearer notes show up next to Cashu in a wallet that isn't mine.

lnurl-server

lnurl-server got about 100 commits. it's an LNURL server that tries to implement all the LUDs, something you run for yourself or friends and family. it is also where the LUD-05 style derivation that LNURLcash reuses was tried out first.

hardware

i also got back into ESP32 stuff. lnurl-vault is a T-Display S3 companion for the wallet that generates secrets and stores notes, and lnurl-card is a design for a credit-card-shaped lightning device: eInk, NFC, a 4×4 keypad, USB-C and no battery.

LNbits and lnurl specs

LNbits got less of my time than in the first half, but it didn't stop. there were commits on lnbits/lnbits, lnurl, satspay, electrum-client, watchonly and the price aggregator.

on lnurl/luds i opened the LUD-25/26 PR, got a small LUD-03 zero balance recommendation merged, and proposed POST callbacks.

small tools

cln-tui, a terminal UI for Core Lightning, got its first release and its own post. dnilabs.com got two articles, and comics got a few commits as a side project.

conclusion

the first half was a mix of a lot of things. Q3 was almost only one thing, and it shows in the numbers. writing a spec, two reference implementations and a conformance suite at the same time is a lot of churn, but every breaking change found a real problem, usually because someone else implemented it differently. in october the focus moved a bit further: hosted mints on run.lnurlcash.com, and formal proofs about the taproot cryptography underneath it in taproot-lean.
